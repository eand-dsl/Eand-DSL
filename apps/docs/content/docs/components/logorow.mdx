## When to use

- **Use** to show the partner brands or benefits that come with a plan, card or highlight:
  up to five logos, then `more` for the rest.
- **Use** `glass` when the row sits on imagery or on a brand-red or midnight surface.
- **Don't use** for the e& brand mark — that is [Logo](./logo). Not for avatars, and not
  for a set of tappable services (use [QuickAction](./quickaction) or
  [ServiceCard](./servicecard)).

## Choosing a size

| `size` | Logo | Gap | Glass tile | Glass gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 24 (`icon/xl`) | 4 | 40 | 4 |
| `md` | 32 (`icon/2xl`) | 4 | 48 | 8 |
| `lg` | 40 (`icon/3xl`) | 8 | 56 | 8 |

`md` is the default because it is the size the row rendered before V1.2; Figma's default is
`sm`. Pass logos as elements sized to fill their circle, or as image URLs. The brand artwork
is not part of the package.

## Props

<PropsTable name="LogoRow" />

The “+N” circle at the end of the row is [MoreItems](./moreitems); `LogoRow` sizes it to
match the logos. Every published logo row uses the `inverse` counter (white glass), which is
built for dark surfaces; `moreSurface` switches it.

## Design

Figma source: [logo-row — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=25997-32516)

- Logos are clipped to circles (`border-radius/9`). The row never wraps.
- Glass tiles: `surface/glass/white/lg` with a backdrop blur from `effects/glass/md/frost`
  (20px) and `spacing/sm` padding. Each logo carries a 0.5px (`border/xs`) inside ring in
  `border/surface-based/glass/default/sm`.
- In RTL the row mirrors and the counter reads “4+”, as `more-items-rtl` does.
