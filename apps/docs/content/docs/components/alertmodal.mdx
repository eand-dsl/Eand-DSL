## When to use

- **Use** at the end of a flow, when the outcome deserves its own screen: an order placed, a
  plan changed, a payment completed. Use `bottom-sheet` when the user should keep sight of the
  page behind.
- **Don't use** to ask for confirmation before an action: in V1.2 this is an outcome screen,
  not a confirmation dialog. For an outcome inside a section or sheet, use
  [PageTitle](./pagetitle). For a brief confirmation, use [Snackbar](./snackbar).

## Anatomy

- **Integration** — `full-screen` (default) fills the viewport on `surface/base/default` and
  scrolls. `bottom-sheet` places the same content on a sheet with 24px top corners, over a
  [Scrim](./scrim).
- **Visual** — `visual`, the hero illustration, framed with a 4px inset.
- **Title block** — `title` in bold 28px and `description` in `body/lg`, both centred.
- **Footer** — `footer`, usually one or two block [Buttons](./button).
- **Close** — `onClose` shows a glass close button at the top end. On the bottom sheet, a scrim
  click also calls it.

```tsx
<AlertModal
  integration="bottom-sheet"
  visual={<img src={banner} alt="" />}
  title="You're all set"
  description="Your new plan starts today."
  footer={<Button block onClick={done}>Done</Button>}
  onClose={done}
/>
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated: the centred dialog">
  When `integration` is unset and `tone`, `body` or `actions` is passed, the V1.0 centred
  confirmation dialog still renders, for existing screens. To move to V1.2, set `integration`,
  and replace `body` with `description`, `actions` with `footer` and `onDismiss` with `onClose`.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [alert-modal — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=32934-67842)

- Title block (`alert-title`): padding 32/16/48 (`spacing/3xl`, `spacing/lg`, `spacing/5xl`),
  gap 4 (`spacing/xs`). The title binds the `typography/heading/lg` variables: bold 28px, 120%,
  tight tracking.
- Footer padding 20 (`spacing/xl`). The close button sits 20px from the top and end, on
  `section-link/glass/default` with a 20px blur.
- The bottom sheet's top corners are 24px (`border-radius/8`).
- Figma's `status-banner` illustrations are not shipped with the library; supply your own
  artwork through `visual`.
