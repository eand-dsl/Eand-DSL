## When to use

- **Use** on existing screens that need free-form content: media of any height, a title, a
  paragraph and an action.
- **Don't use** in new designs where a published card fits: [GeneralCard](./generalcard) for
  general tiles, [PlanCard](./plancard) for plans, [ProductCard](./productcard) for products and
  add-ons, [DealCard](./dealcard) for offers.

## Anatomy

- **Media** — `media` spans the full width at the top; the card clips it to its corners.
- **Content** — a 16px-padded column with a 4px gap: `title` (`title/sm`), `body` (`body/md`,
  subtle), then `children`, then `action` 8px further down.
- **Width** — fills its container by default. Pass `width` to use the card as a carousel cell.

`surface="default"` is white and `surface="inverse"` is midnight-raised. This naming is the
reverse of `GeneralCard`, whose `default` is the dark card, as Figma publishes it.

## Design

Card has no V1.2 symbol. Its nearest published relative is
[General-card — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=28463-12391),
built as `GeneralCard`.

- 20px radius (`border-radius/7`), clipped.
- Content padding 16 (`spacing/lg`), gap 4 (`spacing/xs`).
- Surfaces: `surface/raised/default` (white) or `surface/raised/midnight` (#312c40) with inverse
  ink.
