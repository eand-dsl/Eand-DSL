## When to use

- **Use** to compare plans in a carousel, in a [Section](./section) of size `lg`.
- **Don't use** for products, devices or add-ons (use [ProductCard](./productcard)), or for
  deals with a like count (use [DealCard](./dealcard)).

## Anatomy

- **Header** — `category`, the overline, in `body/md` subtle; `name` in bold 20px, up to two
  lines; then the logo row of 32px partner logos with a +N bubble. Pass `logos` and `moreLogos`,
  or leave `smiles` on for placeholders.
- **Footer** — bottom-aligned: the `lg` Discount badge (`discount={true}` labels it
  "Discount"), the "from" line (`from`), then `price` and `period`.

`category` and `period` accept `null` to hide them.

`colorScheme="default"` is the white card; `colorScheme="inverse"` is the midnight card with
white ink. Both use the same red Discount badge.

```tsx
<PlanCard name="Entertainment plans" category="Postpaid" price="AED 1250" period="/mo" discount="20% off" />
<PlanCard colorScheme="inverse" name="Data plans" discount={false} from={false} price="AED 300" />
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  `variant` is now `colorScheme`: `midnight` becomes `inverse`. `brand` has no Figma
  counterpart.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [plans-portable — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=26003-40647)

- 222 × 300 (`section/body/height/row-4`) at a 16px radius (`border-radius/6`), padding 16
  (`spacing/lg`), on `surface/base/inverse` or `surface/base/midnight`.
- Header and footer gaps are 4 (`spacing/xs`); the logo row has 8px above it (`spacing/sm`).
- The name applies the `heading/md` text style: 20px at 110%. The price is `title/md`.
- Inverse ink binds `text/default/inverse` and `text/default/inverse-subtle`. The +N bubble is
  `surface/glass/white/md` on the inverse card and `surface/glass/midnight/lg` on the default one.
