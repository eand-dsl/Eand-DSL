## When to use

- **Use** for a focused task or detail that keeps the user in context: choosing an option,
  reviewing details before paying, reading a system message.
- **Don't use** for a whole flow (use a page), for the outcome of a flow (use
  [AlertModal](./alertmodal) with `integration="bottom-sheet"`), or for a passing confirmation
  (use [Snackbar](./snackbar)).

## Choosing a type

| `type` | Figma composition |
| --- | --- |
| `basic` | Visual, icon, title and description |
| `detailed` (default) | Title and body |
| `system-message` | Close button and body, no grabber |

`type` records the Figma variant and sets the grabber default: on, except for
`system-message`. The content you pass decides what renders.

## Anatomy

- **Grabber** — a 40 × 4 pill at the top. `grabber` overrides the type's default.
- **Visual** — `visual`, media above the header, framed with a 4px inset and a 16px radius.
- **Title block** — an `icon` in a 40px tile, the `title` in bold 20px, and a `description` in
  `body/md`.
- **Pills** — `pills`, a row of [Chips](./chip) 16px under the title that scrolls sideways.
- **Body** — `children`, in a scrolling content area on `surface/base/default`.
- **Footer** — `footer`, for example block buttons or a [CTA Bar](./ctabar).

Setting `onDismiss` shows a close button and lets a scrim click dismiss the sheet. The close
sits at the end of the title row, or over the visual when there is one. `closeButton={false}`
hides it.

```tsx
<BottomSheet
  title="Choose a plan"
  description="Prices include VAT."
  pills={<><Chip selected>Monthly</Chip><Chip>Yearly</Chip></>}
  onDismiss={close}
  footer={<Button block onClick={confirm}>Continue</Button>}
>
  …
</BottomSheet>
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  `display="dark"` has no V1.2 counterpart; the sheet is always light. `subheader` is replaced by
  `pills`; it still renders under the title.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [bottom-sheet — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=40299-160405)

- Sheet: `surface/sunken/inverse` (#f0f0f5) with 24px top corners (`border-radius/8`), on a
  [Scrim](./scrim): `surface/overlay/scrim` under a 20px blur.
- Title frame padding 20/20/0 (`spacing/xl`); the title binds `font/size/6` (20px) at 110%;
  the header ends with 20px of padding.
- Close: a 40px section-link button, solid in the title row and glass inverse 20px in from the
  top end over a visual.
- Body padding 0/20/20 on `surface/base/default`; footer padding 20.
- Grabber: 40 × 4 on `surface/glass/midnight/md`, in an 8px frame.
