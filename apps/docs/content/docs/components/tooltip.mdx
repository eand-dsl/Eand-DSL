## When to use

- **Use** to explain a control or a term in place, or for a guided hint during onboarding (the
  rich size, with `steps`).
- **Don't use** for information the user needs to complete the task; put it on the page. Not
  for status (use [Alert](./alert)) or for long content (use [BottomSheet](./bottomsheet)).

## Choosing a size

| `size` | Width | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `simple` | Hugs one line | `content` only |
| `standard` (default) | 200px | `content` as a short paragraph |
| `rich` | 280px | `title`, `media`, `content`, then `steps` and an `action`; `onClose` adds a close button |

`surface` is `default` (white) or `dark` (midnight).

## Placement and caret

- `placement` is the side of the target the bubble sits on: `top` (default), `bottom`, `left`
  or `right`. The caret always points back at the target.
- `caretAlign` moves the caret along the edge: `start`, `center` (default) or `end`, inset 32px
  from the corner. It applies to `top` and `bottom`; on the sides the caret stays centred.
- `caret={false}` hides it.

Visibility is controlled: the tooltip shows while `visible` is true. Wire it to your own hover,
focus or tap handling.

```tsx
<Tooltip
  size="rich"
  visible={open}
  placement="bottom"
  caretAlign="start"
  title="Track your usage"
  content="See what is left of your data, minutes and roaming."
  steps="1 of 3"
  action={<Button size="sm">Next</Button>}
  onClose={() => setOpen(false)}
>
  <Icon name="info" size={20} />
</Tooltip>
```

## Design

Figma source: [Tooltip — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=33202-6649)

- Simple: 4px radius (`border-radius/1`), padding 4/12. Standard: 12px radius
  (`border-radius/4`), padding 12. Rich: 20px radius (`border-radius/7`), padding 16.
- Surface `surface/canvas/default` or `surface/canvas/midnight`; every size carries `shadow/md`
  (0 0 44px, `midnight/a10`).
- Caret: a 12 × 6 shape on `surface/raised/default` (or `surface/canvas/midnight` when dark),
  inset by `spacing/3xl` when it is not centred.
- Rich: the media clips to a 20px radius; the body is `body/sm` in `text/default/subtle`; the
  close is a 32px button at a 12px radius on `surface/base/default`, holding a 17px
  `close-filled`.
