## When to use

- **Use** for status that belongs to a place in the page and stays there: a plan about to expire,
  a payment that failed, a note about a service. One call to action per alert.
- **Don't use** for a passing confirmation (use [Snackbar](./snackbar)), for the outcome of a
  flow (use [PageTitle](./pagetitle) or [AlertModal](./alertmodal)), or for form validation
  (use the [Input](./input) error state).

## Choosing a status

| `status` | Surface | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `success` | `status-indicator/surface/positive` | Something completed |
| `alert` | `status-indicator/surface/warning` (amber) | Needs attention soon |
| `warning` | `status-indicator/surface/danger` (orange) | A problem to resolve |
| `info` (default) | `status-indicator/surface/neutral` | Neutral information |
| `info-midnight` | `alert-message/surface/midnight` | Information on dark screens |

Figma's `alert` is the amber step and its `warning` the orange one. V1.2 has no red alert.

## Anatomy

- **Tile** — a 32px tile in the status colour, holding a 16px white filled glyph.
- **Text** — `title` in `title/xs` and `children` in `body/md`. Both use the default ink on every
  status except `info-midnight`, which is white.
- **Action** — `action` renders a primary `sm` inverse [Button](./button) that calls `onAction`.
  `actionPlacement="below"` moves it under the text, for a body that runs to several lines.
- **Stack** — when several alerts share one slot, `stack` (the count) and `stackIndex` (the one
  in view, from 0) draw a vertical step counter beside the box.

```tsx
<Alert status="alert" title="Your plan renews in 3 days" action="Review" onAction={review}>
  AED 250 will be charged to your card.
</Alert>

<Alert status="info" stack={3} stackIndex={0}>Roaming is on for this trip.</Alert>
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  `tone` is now `status`: `positive` becomes `success`, `warning` becomes `alert`, `danger`
  becomes `warning` and `default` becomes `info`.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [alert-message — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=40289-34516)

- Box: padding 8 (`spacing/sm`), gap 12 (`spacing/md`), 16px radius (`border-radius/6`).
- Tile: 32px at a 12px radius (`border-radius/4`), padding 6, on `status/positive`,
  `status/warning`, `status/danger` or `status/default`; `info-midnight` uses
  `surface/glass/white/md`.
- Text: `title/xs` and `body/md` in `text/default/default`, or `text/default/inverse` on
  `info-midnight`. The ink no longer follows the status.
- Step counter: 5px dots on `midnight/400`, 4px apart; the dot in view is 5 × 12 with a midnight
  head.
