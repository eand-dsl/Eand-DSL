## When to use

- **Use** `primary` for the one main action on a screen or sheet, `secondary` beside it for
  the alternative, and `tertiary` or `link` for low-emphasis text actions. Use `glass` on
  imagery and brand surfaces.
- **Use** `block` for full-width actions, such as those in a [CTA Bar](./ctabar).
- **Don't use** for the legal links in a consent row ([ConsentCheckbox](./consentcheckbox)
  sets them), for filters ([Chip](./chip), [FilterPill](./filterpill)) or for a section's
  “See all” ([SectionLink](./sectionlink)).

## Choosing a scheme

| `scheme` | Primary, tertiary, link | Secondary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `default` | Brand red | Red outline |
| `inverse` | White, for red and dark backgrounds | Dark (midnight) outline |
| `midnight` | Ink | White outline |

Secondary follows the published symbol, whose bindings are crossed relative to every other
type: `scheme="inverse"` is the dark outline and `scheme="midnight"` the white one. On a red or
dark background, pair `primary scheme="inverse"` with `secondary scheme="midnight"`. Whether
the crossing is intended is an open design question. Glass has no scheme.

## Sizes and states

- **Sizes** — `lg` 48, `md` 40, `sm` 32. The icon slots are 24 / 20 / 16, so pass
  `<Icon size>` 20 / 16 / 12 to `leadingIcon` and `trailingIcon`.
- **Tertiary and link** hug their text, with no height or padding. Link is set in body type
  and is underlined only while pressed.
- **Pressed** follows the pointer, or Space and Enter while held; it never lingers on focus.
  Keyboard focus shows a 2px ring. Figma has no focus state, so the ring is a code-side
  accessibility addition.
- **Disabled** uses the disabled tokens, with no opacity. **Loading** puts a spinner in the
  leading slot and suppresses clicks.

`surface` and `tone` are deprecated aliases from V1.1; `scheme` wins when both are set.
Rendering is identical except for secondary:

| Deprecated | New (primary, tertiary, link, glass) | New (secondary) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `surface="brand"` | `scheme="default"` | `scheme="default"` |
| `surface="inverse-brand"`, `"white"`, `tone="inverse"` | `scheme="inverse"` | `scheme="midnight"` |
| `surface="midnight"` | `scheme="midnight"` | `scheme="inverse"` |

## Design

Figma source: [button — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=25394-85067)

- Height and minimum width `scale/48·40·32`; radius `border-radius/9`.
- Inline inset 16 / 16 / 10: the outer `spacing/sm` (`spacing/xs` at `sm`), plus the content's
  `spacing/xs`, plus the label's `spacing/xs` (`spacing/2xs` at `sm`).
- Secondary has a 1px (`border/sm`) inside stroke; pressed is a solid fill with no stroke.
- Labels are `button/lg·md·sm`; link uses `body/lg·md·sm`. Glass is `button/glass/*` behind a
  20px backdrop blur.
