## When to use

- **Use** to show the balance of one allowance — data, minutes, roaming — in a plan breakdown.
  Stack bars with a 4px gap, one per allowance.
- **Don't use** for progress through a task or flow (use [ProgressBar](./progressbar) or
  [Stepper](./stepper)), or for a loyalty tier (use [SmilesBalance](./smilesbalance)).

## Anatomy

A 48px rounded row where a coloured block shows how much of the allowance is **left**. The
amount reads inside the block; the category `label` sits in the track at the opposite end.

- **Value** — the amount, the `unit` and "left", with an optional `note` underneath, such as
  "Expires in 3 days".
- **Label** — the category, end-aligned.

`remaining` is what the block measures, not consumption. Pass `total` so the fill has something
to be a share of; with `total={0}` there is no allowance and nothing is drawn.

`status` is explicit rather than derived: `default` fills green, `low-data` orange. The point at
which an allowance counts as low is a product rule that differs for data, minutes and roaming,
so the bar does not guess it.

## Text on the fill

Where the block sits under the text, the text switches to its `filled` state: white, and for
the value a Medium weight. The bar measures this itself, separately for the value and the
label: each flips once the fill covers it completely. Pin either one with `valueState` or
`labelState` when you need a fixed result, for example in a static mock-up.

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  `used` still works and is converted to `remaining` as `total - used`. Pass `remaining`.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [.plan-usage-bar — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=28927-23669) ·
[states](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=33838-4470)

- Track: 48px tall, 12px radius (`border-radius/4`), on `surface/canvas/inverse`, with 16px
  side padding (`spacing/lg`).
- Fill: the `Progress status` axis
  ([28927:22814](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=28927-22814)),
  green #54bc72 or orange #ff9f52. Two discrete states, not a gradient.
- Value: `title/xs` in `text/default/default`; filled, `button/md` in `text/default/inverse`.
  The amount, unit and "left" are spaced by 2px (`spacing/2xs`). Note: `body/sm` at 130%.
- Label: `button/md`, in `text/default/default` or, filled, `text/default/inverse`.

The bar is one part of the Figma **plan-usage** card
([38351:53904](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=38351-53904)).
That card is a screen pattern, not a component; assemble it from the parts: a row of outline
[Chips](./chip) (40px, 4px apart) and the stacked bars (4px apart) in a 20px-radius card on
`surface/canvas/default` with 12px padding and an 8px gap, followed after 4px by a 72px
[ActionBar](./actionbar) prompting a top-up.
