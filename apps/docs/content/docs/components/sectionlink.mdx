<Callout title="No V1.2 symbol">
  Figma DSL V1.2 publishes no standalone title and "See all" row. The published equivalent is
  the header of [Section](./section), with its `title` and `trigger`.
</Callout>

## When to use

- **Use** only outside a `Section`, where a custom layout needs a heading with a text link.
- **Don't use** inside a `Section`: its header already provides the title and the trigger, as a
  chevron button or a "See all" button.

## Anatomy

- **Title** — `title` in `heading/xs`.
- **Link** — `link` (default "See all") in `button/sm` and `text/brand/default`, followed by a
  16px `chevron-right-sm`. It calls `onLinkClick`.

The row is 40px tall, with the title and link at opposite ends.
