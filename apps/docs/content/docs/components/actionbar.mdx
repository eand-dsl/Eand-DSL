## When to use

- **Use** for a row the user acts on: a settings entry, a prompt to top up, a shortcut into a
  flow, a feature toggle. Figma points tappable rows here rather than to the list item.
- **Don't use** for read-only data such as a balance or a price (use [ListRow](./listrow)), or
  for a grid of shortcuts (use [QuickAction](./quickaction)).

## Anatomy

- **Leading** (optional) — `icon`, an icon or image in a 40px tile.
- **Text** — `title` in `title/xs` and `subtitle` in `body/md`, both in the scheme ink, each a
  single line that truncates.
- **Trigger** — one of:
  - `chevron`: a trailing chevron; the whole bar becomes the tap target, handled by `onClick`.
  - `action`: a trailing node, typically a [Button](./button).
  - `switcher`: a [Switcher](./switcher); pass `true` or the `Switcher` props.
- **Stack** — `stack` adds two glass layers that peek out below the bar, for a pile of
  related prompts. It renders with the inverse scheme only.

## Choosing a surface

`colorScheme` sets the ink. When it is unset, it follows the surface: the glass and midnight
surfaces and `white-transparent` are inverse, the rest default.

| Family | `surface` |
| --- | --- |
| Default ink | `default` (`surface/raised/default`), `subtle` (`surface/base/default`), `sunken` (`surface/sunken/default`), `glass` with `colorScheme="default"` |
| Inverse ink | `glass` (20% white with a blur), `white-transparent` (20% white), `midnight-base`, `midnight-raised`, `midnight-transparent` |

```tsx
<ActionBar
  surface="midnight-raised"
  title="Running low on data"
  subtitle="Add 5 GB for AED 25"
  action={<Button size="sm">Top up</Button>}
/>

<ActionBar title="Wi-Fi calling" subtitle="Call over any network" switcher={{ defaultChecked: true }} />
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated and changed">
  `inverse` is deprecated; use `colorScheme="inverse"`. `white-transparent` moved to the
  inverse family in V1.2, so it now renders white ink.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [action-bar — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=28201-38278)

- 72px tall (`scale/72`), 16px side padding (`spacing/lg`), 20px radius (`border-radius/7`),
  12px gap (`spacing/md`).
- Leading tile: 40px at `border-radius/4`, on `surface/base/default`, or `white/a15` on the
  inverse scheme.
- The description binds `text/default/default` (or `text/default/inverse`), the same ink as the
  title, not a subtle step.
- The chevron is a 20px glyph in a 24px box.
- Stack: two 64px layers at a 16px radius (`border-radius/6`) on `surface/glass/white/md` with a
  blur, inset 6 and 12px, each peeking 10px below the one above, for a 92px total.
