## When to use

- **Use** for offers in a "Deals for you" carousel, in a [Section](./section) of size `md`.
- **Don't use** for plans (use [PlanCard](./plancard)), for products and add-ons (use
  [ProductCard](./productcard)), or for picture-led announcements (use [NewCard](./newcard)).

## Anatomy

- **Header** — `overline` in `body/sm` subtle, then `title` in bold 16px, up to two lines. The
  header takes the free height, so removing the logo row moves nothing else.
- **Logos** — `logos`, the small logo row; `true` shows placeholders.
- **Footer** — bottom-aligned, in a fixed 80px:
  - `discount`, the `md` Discount badge, beside `wasPrice`, the old price struck through;
  - `price` in `title/md`;
  - a feedback pill with thumbs up, the `likes` count and thumbs down. It appears when `likes`,
    `onLike` or `onDislike` is set.

```tsx
<DealCard
  overline="Smiles"
  title="20% off your next dinner"
  logos
  discount="20% off"
  wasPrice="AED 500"
  price="AED 400"
  likes="120.8k"
  onLike={like}
  onDislike={dislike}
/>
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  The V1.1 deal card was an image card. `subtitle` is now `overline` and `badge` is now
  `discount` (a label). `image` still renders, in the logo-row slot, but the V1.2 deals card has
  no image.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [deals-card — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=25973-22611)

- 166 × 224 (`section/body/width/col-12` × `row-3`) at a 16px radius (`border-radius/6`), padding
  12 (`spacing/md`), on `surface/base/inverse`. No border, no image.
- The title applies the `heading/xs` text style: 16px at 120%.
- Logo row 48px tall, including 12px below; footer 80px, gap 4 (`spacing/xs`).
- Feedback pill: 24px tall on `surface/glass/midnight/sm`, with a 1px divider in
  `border/surface-based/canvas/inverse` between the thumbs.
- The price binds a legacy ink (#140f21) that has no V1.2 token, so it is a literal.
