## When to use

- **Use** for the outcome of an action — payment success, a failed top-up, a plan change that
  needs attention — on a full page, inside a `Section`, or inside a `BottomSheet`.
- **Don't use** to introduce a page; that is [PageHeader](./pageheader). Not for inline form
  validation or toasts (use `Input` errors and `Snackbar`).

## Choosing a size

| Type | Title | Icon | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lg` | 24px bold | yes | Top-level outcomes such as a payment confirmation |
| `md` | 20px bold | yes | Outcomes inside a section or a tall sheet |
| `sm` | 18px bold | no | Messages lower in the hierarchy |
| `xs` | 16px bold | no | Compact messages inside a sheet or card |

Use `alignment="center"` for standalone screens and `"left"` when the message sits in a content
flow. Always match `status` to the real outcome: `positive`, `danger`, `warning`, `brand` (an etisalat
moment, not success) or `neutral`.

## Design

Figma source: [page-title — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=40283-145186) ·
[status-icon](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=40283-145559)

- Gap 12 (`spacing/md`), title-to-description 4 (`spacing/xs`), bottom padding 20 (`spacing/xl`).
- Title sizes are bound to `font/size/7·6·5` and `heading/xs` — a step below the `heading/*`
  text styles of the same names. Line height 110% (120% at `xs`); the title clamps at two lines.
- Status tile: `status-icon/surface/<type>` behind a 32px filled glyph in `status-icon/icon/<type>`,
  `border-radius/7`.
