## When to use

- **Use** at the end of a row of logos to say how many are not shown. [LogoRow](./logorow) adds
  one for you with `more={n}`, sized to its logos; use `MoreItems` directly only when you compose
  a row yourself.
- **Don't use** for notification or unread counts; that is [Badge](./badge).

## Surfaces and size

- `surface="default"` is midnight glass, for light backgrounds. `surface="inverse"` is white
  glass, for dark backgrounds and imagery; on a light surface it is close to invisible.
- Every published logo-row uses `inverse`, so `LogoRow` defaults `moreSurface` to it. Whether
  logo rows on light surfaces should switch to `default` is an open design question.
- Set `size` to the diameter of the logos beside it (24–56px). The default is 24px (`icon/xl`).
- In RTL the circle reads "N+", as the published `more-items-rtl` does.

## Design

Figma source: [more-items — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=26560-27420) ·
[more-items-rtl](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=37905-16028)

- A clipped circle at `border-radius/9`.
- Fill: `surface/glass/midnight/lg` (default) or `surface/glass/white/md` (inverse).
- "+" and the count are two text cells in `typography/body/xs` (10px Regular),
  `text/default/inverse`, centred. Flex order reverses them under `dir="rtl"`.
