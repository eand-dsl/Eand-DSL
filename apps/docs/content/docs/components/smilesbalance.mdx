## When to use

- **Use** at the top of the Smiles area or the account hub, to show the member's balance and how
  close they are to the next tier.
- **Don't use** to list partner brands (use [SmilesRow](./smilesrow)) or for plan allowances
  (use [PlanUsageBar](./planusagebar)).

## Anatomy

- **Total** — `points` in bold 28px, over the `label` ("Points") in bold 24px.
- **Info** — `tooltip` (on by default) adds an info button after the label; `onInfo` handles it.
- **Tier pill** — the current `tier` (`silver`, `gold` or `platinum`) with its medal. `null`
  hides it.
- **Footer** — a progress bar (`progress`, 0 to 100) over a line that names `pointsToNext` and
  the `nextTier`. `message` replaces the line.

`active={false}` is the not-yet-activated state: no tier pill, and the line invites the member
to activate, with `pointsToNext` as the reward.

```tsx
<SmilesBalance points="40,340" tier="silver" nextTier="gold" pointsToNext="2,500" progress={40} onInfo={explain} />
<SmilesBalance active={false} points="0" pointsToNext="500" />
```

## Design

Figma source: [smiles-balance-card — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=27131-101113)

- Full width × 148px (`row-2`), 16px radius (`border-radius/6`), on `surface/base/inverse`.
- Header padding 16/16/0 (`spacing/lg`). The total binds the `typography/heading/lg` variables
  (28px, 120%); "Points" applies the `heading/lg` text style (24px, 110%).
- Tier pill: 40px tall, a 32px medal and the tier name.
- Progress bar: 12px tall, a `surface/sunken/inverse` track and a `text/positive/subtle` fill.
- Several bindings are legacy styles with no V1.2 token (the ink, the pill fill and the tier
  gradients), so they are literals. Only the Silver medal exists in Figma; Gold and Platinum
  reuse their tier gradient.
