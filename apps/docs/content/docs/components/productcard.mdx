## When to use

- **Use** for packages and add-ons, products such as devices and accessories, and shop
  categories, in a [Section](./section) of size `lg`.
- **Don't use** for plans (use [PlanCard](./plancard)) or for deals (use
  [DealCard](./dealcard)).

## Choosing a type

| `type` | Tinted panel | Footer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `package` | 176px: `eyebrow`, `title` (up to four lines), logo row | Discount or swatches, price |
| `product` | 144px: a 112px `image`, with the logo row over it | `title` (two lines), discount or swatches, price |
| `category` | Fills the card: `title` (one line), a 128px `image`, the Discount badge and the price | — |

Without a `type`, a card with an `image` is a `product` and one without is a `package`.

## Anatomy

- **Tint** — `tint` colours the panel only; the card itself is always white. Pass a
  `.card-bg-color` name (`default`, `red`, `orange`, `yellow`, `green`, `cyan`, `blue`,
  `violet`) or any CSS colour.
- **Discount or swatches** — `discount` renders the `lg` Discount badge; `colors` renders 12px
  colour spots in its place.
- **Price** — a label, then `price` and `period` in `body/xl`. `priceLabel` is `from` (default),
  `pay-only`, `smiles-combined` (with `labelPts`, "From 30 PTS") or `none`. `pts` shows the
  price in Smiles points.
- **Logos** — `logos` adds the small logo row on `package` and `product` cards; `true` shows
  placeholders.

```tsx
<ProductCard type="package" eyebrow="Add-on" title="Unlimited social data" discount="20% off" price="AED 35" />
<ProductCard type="product" image={<img src={phone} alt="" />} title="iPhone 16" colors={['#4fa0f8', '#b3b3b3']} priceLabel="pay-only" />
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated and changed">
  `type="addon"` is deprecated; it is the V1.1 name of `package`. `tint` used to colour the
  whole card and now colours the panel only.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [card-features-package — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=37905-18719) ·

[card-features-category](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=37905-18788)

- 229 × 300 (`section/body/height/row-4`) at a 20px radius (`border-radius/7`) on
  `surface/raised/default`.
- The tint panel has a 24px radius (`border-radius/8`) and a 4px inside ring in
  `border/interactive/inverse/pressed` (white).
- Footer padding 8/12/12 (`spacing/sm`, `spacing/md`), gap 4, bottom-aligned.
- Titles apply the heading text styles: `heading/sm` (18px, 110%) on package and product,
  `heading/md` (20px) on category. The price is `body/xl` (20px Regular) under a `body/md`
  subtle label.
- The product title binds a legacy ink (#140f21) that has no V1.2 token, so it is a literal.
