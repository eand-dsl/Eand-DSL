## Anatomy

`TopBar` composes the Figma master's slots:

- **Top row** — `leading` (back / avatar), `logo` or `title` in the middle, and `actions` + `trailing` on the right (circular glass buttons on brand).
- **Big-title block** — `eyebrow` (overline) · `bigTitle` (`heading.lg`) · `subtext`, with an optional `chevron`.
- **Account display** — `account={{ greeting, name }}` for the masked-number home header.
- **Bottom slot** — anything passed as `children`: a `Searchbar`, `Chip` row, `Tabs`, `Stepper`, or action cards.

`surface` is `brand` (red hero, rounded bottom, `statusBar` on by default) or `default` (compact 56px light bar).
