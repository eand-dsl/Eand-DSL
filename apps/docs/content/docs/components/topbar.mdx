## When to use

- **Use** once per screen, at the top. Home and hub screens take `surface="brand"` with an
  `account` or a `bigTitle`. Detail and pushed pages take `onBack` and a `title`, on `white`
  over a white canvas or `none` over a grey or midnight one.
- **Don't use** for headings inside the page (use [PageHeader](./pageheader) below the bar, or
  [Section](./section)), or for the title of a sheet (use [BottomSheet](./bottomsheet)).

## Choosing a surface

| `surface` | Fill | Header scheme |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brand` | `surface/base/brand` | White ink, glass controls, white logo |
| `midnight` | `surface/base/midnight` | White ink, glass controls, white logo |
| `white` | `surface/base/inverse` | Midnight ink, grey controls, red logo |
| `none` | Transparent | Midnight ink, white controls, red logo |

Without a `surface`, the bar is `brand` when it has an `account` or `bigTitle`, and `white`
otherwise. A title-only `<TopBar title="…" />` is the Figma page header: a white card with the
status bar and a centred page name.

## Anatomy

- **Status bar** — a 62px faux iOS bar, on by default. `statusBar={false}` removes it.
- **Header row** — three columns; the middle stays centred whatever the sides hold.
  - **Left:** `onBack` renders the 40px back button; `leading` takes custom content; `account`
    shows an optional 40px `avatar`, a `greeting` in `body/md` and the masked number in
    `title/sm` with a 16px chevron (`chevron={false}` hides it).
  - **Middle:** `logo` (`true` renders the etisalat lockup, white or red to suit the surface), or a
    `title` in `title/sm` with an optional `subtitle` in `body/md`.
  - **Right:** `actions`, each wrapped in a 40px round control (pass icons), then `trailing`.
- **Title block** — `eyebrow` in `body/md` (`eyebrowChevron` adds a 16px chevron), `bigTitle`
  in bold 24px (`chevron` adds a 24px chevron after it), and `subtext` in `body/lg`.
- **Add-ons** — `children`, such as a [Searchbar](./searchbar), [Chip](./chip) row,
  [Tabs](./tabs) or [Stepper](./stepper), each with 16px above and at the sides.

```tsx
<TopBar
  surface="brand"
  account={{ greeting: 'Good morning, Sara', name: '050 123 4567' }}
  logo
  actions={[<Icon key="alerts" name="notification" size={20} />]}
/>

<TopBar surface="white" onBack={() => history.back()} title="Usage" subtitle="050 123 4567" />
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  - `surface="default"` is now `surface="white"`, and `variant` is now `surface`.
  - `greeting` is now `account={{ greeting }}`.
  - `actionBar` is replaced by a card passed as `children`.
  - `compact` renders a code-only 56px row with no status bar. Figma has no compact top bar,
    so use the default header with `onBack` and `title` instead.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [topbar — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=38172-12163)

- Every surface is an inset card: 4px around the top and sides (`spacing/xs`), a 36px top
  radius and a 20px bottom radius (`border-radius/7`), 16px bottom padding. The 36px radius has
  no token, so it is a literal. `rounded={false}` squares all four corners.
- The status bar is 62px tall with 15/24/19/24 padding, the values of the iOS kit Figma uses.
- Header row: 40px minimum (`scale/40`), 16px side padding (`spacing/lg`), on a
  `minmax(0, 1fr) auto minmax(0, 1fr)` grid.
- Controls follow the header scheme: 20% white glass with a blur on `brand` and `midnight`,
  solid #f0f0f5 on `white`, solid white on `none`.
- The large title applies the `heading/lg` text style: 24px, 110%, snug tracking, with 16/16/0
  padding around the block.
