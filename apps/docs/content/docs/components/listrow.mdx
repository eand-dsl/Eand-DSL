## When to use

- **Use** to present one piece of data: a balance, an add-on and its price, a line on a bill,
  a setting's current value. Inside a [Section](./section), rows stack 4px apart.
- **Don't use** as a tap target. Figma defines the list item as a data row and points tappable
  rows to [ActionBar](./actionbar). Not for section headings (use [Section](./section)).

<Callout title="A div, not a button">
  `ListRow` renders a `<div>`. Passing `onClick` still renders a `<button>` so existing screens
  keep working, but new tappable rows should use `ActionBar`.
</Callout>

## Anatomy

- **Leading** (optional) — `icon` in a 40px tile on `surface/glass/midnight/sm`, or `logo`
  full-bleed in a 40px tile on `surface/base/default`. Both tiles have a 12px radius.
- **Content** — `label` in `title/xs`; `tooltip` adds an info glyph after it (`onTooltipClick`
  makes the glyph a button); `sublabel` in `body/md`, subtle; `action` below, typically a
  tertiary `md` [Button](./button).
- **Trailing** — end-aligned: `value` in `title/xs`, `hint` in `body/md` subtle, and `badge`.
  Then `chevron` (on by default), a 16px `chevron-right`. Set `chevron={false}` for pure data.

## Choosing a type

| `type` | Layout | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `info` (default) | Padded card, 16px all round, on a `surface` | Standalone data rows |
| `price` | Flat, 16px bottom padding, no surface | Line items in a price summary |

`padding` overrides the type's default.

Pass `inverse` on dark or glass backgrounds: the title and value turn white, the subtitle and
hint inverse-subtle, and the icon tile `surface/glass/white/sm`. The `surface` options follow
the scheme:

- **Light** — `default` (`surface/raised/default`), `subtle` (`surface/base/default`), `sunken`
  (`surface/sunken/default`), `glass`.
- **Inverse** — `glass` (the default when `inverse` is set), `midnight-base`,
  `midnight-raised`, `midnight-transparent`, `white-transparent`.

```tsx
<ListRow type="price" label="Data add-on" sublabel="10 GB, 30 days" value="AED 49.00" chevron={false} />
<ListRow inverse surface="midnight-raised" label="Balance" tooltip value="AED 120.50" hint="Due 12 Oct" />
```

## Design

Figma source: [list-item — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=34677-66964)

- Padded rows: 16px padding (`spacing/lg`) at a 20px radius (`border-radius/7`).
- Gaps: 12 between leading, content and trailing (`spacing/md`); 8 between the tile and the
  text (`spacing/sm`); 2 inside the content and trailing blocks (`spacing/2xs`).
- Leading tile 40px (`scale/40`) at `border-radius/4`, holding a 32px icon box; it stays
  top-aligned when the content runs to several lines.
- Title and value `title/xs` (14px); subtitle and hint `body/md` (14px) in
  `text/default/subtle`.
- The chevron sits in a 20px box, 4px after the value block, vertically centred.
