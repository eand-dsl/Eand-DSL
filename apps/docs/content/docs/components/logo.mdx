## When to use

- **The symbol** (no `type`) wherever the brand needs to appear: headers, splash screens,
  cards. `default` is the red square tile with a white lockup; use `white` on brand-red,
  midnight or image backgrounds, and `red` on light surfaces.
- **A bare artwork** (`type`) when the space calls for a specific form: `logomark` for tight
  spots such as a small avatar, `wordmark` for text-only placements, `lockup`, or
  `bilingual` where English and Arabic must both appear.
- Pass `rtl` on Arabic screens: the lockup and wordmark switch to the Arabic artwork.
  Logomark and bilingual have no separate Arabic version.
- **Not for** partner or app logos — use [LogoRow](./logorow) with the partner's own artwork.

## Design

Figma source: [Etisalat Logo — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=27020-6155)

- `etisalat-logo` (27032:50455): a 96 × 96 symbol, color-scheme default | white | red. The
  default tile is `color/red/1000` with square corners. The lockup spans 136/192 of the width.
- `.etisalat-logo-variations` (42176:33598): lockup, logomark, wordmark and bilingual, each
  edge to edge in its frame. Figma spells the last one `billingual`.
- V1.2 replaced the V1.1 "e&" lockup and dropped its `midnight` version. `version="midnight"`
  still renders, in midnight ink, but is deprecated.
