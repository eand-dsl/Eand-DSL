## When to use

- **Use** at the top of a content page, directly below the top bar, to set the page's theme
  and give immediate category filtering. One per screen, outside any `Section`.
- **Don't use** for the result of an action (success, failure, confirmation) — that is
  [PageTitle](./pagetitle). Not for headings inside a page (use `Section`), and not for sheet
  or modal titles.

## Anatomy

- **Icon** — a 56px slot, typically a 3D illustration. Do not resize it.
- **Title** — bold 28px at 120%, up to two lines. Each line pairs a *muted* word with a *strong* word:
  `{ muted: 'Discover', strong: 'Thermostats.' }`.
- **Subtitle** — `body/md`, centred. Optional but recommended.
- **Tabs** — 48px pills in a row that scrolls sideways. Exactly one is always selected.

Pass `inverse` on dark or image backgrounds: the card becomes 20% white glass and every text
token flips to its inverse. The tab pills follow the header — don't restyle them yourself.

## Design

Figma source: [page-header — e& Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=37390-28734)

- Card padding 24 × 16 (`spacing/2xl`, `spacing/lg`); content gap 12 (`spacing/md`); title-to-subtitle 4.
- The title binds the `typography/heading/lg` variables (28px, 120%, tight tracking), not the
  24px `heading/lg` text style that `Section` applies.
- Default surface is `surface/base/inverse` (white) at `border-radius/7` (20px); inverse is
  `surface/glass/white/lg` at `border-radius/8` (24px), as the published symbol has them.
- In RTL the title words, subtitle and tab row mirror automatically.
