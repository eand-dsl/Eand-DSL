## When to use

- **Use** wherever a person signs in, or continues a flow, with UAE PASS, the national digital
  identity.
- **Don't use** for any other sign-in method, or as a general outlined button; that is
  [Button](./button). Keep the logo and the copy as published.

## Choosing a variant

| `variant` | Label | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `login` | Login with UAE PASS | Logo before the text |
| `continue` | Continue with UAE PASS | Logo before the text |
| `rtl` | تسجيل الدخول بالهوية الرقمية | Sets `dir="rtl"` and `lang="ar"` on the button; the logo follows the text |

It is a separate component, not a `Button` variant, because Figma publishes it as its own set: one
48px size, a brand logo instead of icon slots, fixed copy, and no `scheme`, pressed or loading
state. `block` stretches it to the container width. `disabled` switches the stroke and label to
their disabled tokens and dims the logo. `children` replaces the label; leave it unset to keep the
published copy. The button defaults to `type="button"` and forwards its ref.

## Design

Figma source: [button-uae-pass — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=39861-22282)

- 48px tall (`scale/48`), at least 48 wide, inline padding 12 (`spacing/md`), `border-radius/9`,
  no fill.
- A 1px inside stroke in `button/secondary/text/midnight/default`; disabled,
  `button/secondary/border/brand/disabled`.
- Label `button/lg` in `button/secondary/text/midnight/default` (`…/disabled` when disabled), after
  a 24px logo, with `spacing/xs` padding on the content and on the label.
- The logo is the UAE PASS mark exported from Figma, in its own colours rather than etisalat tokens.
  Disabled, it drops to 34% opacity, a value Figma does not bind to a token.
