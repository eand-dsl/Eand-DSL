## When to use

- **Use** to confirm, briefly and without blocking, the result of something the user just did:
  a payment received, an add-on activated. Use `loader` while a short process runs.
- **Don't use** for status that must stay on the page (use [Alert](./alert)), or for an
  outcome that deserves its own screen (use [AlertModal](./alertmodal) or
  [PageTitle](./pagetitle)).

## Choosing a status

| `status` | Tile | Glyph |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `success` | `status-indicator/surface/positive` | `check-circle-filled` |
| `alert` | `status-indicator/surface/warning` (amber) | `warning-filled` |
| `warning` | `status-indicator/surface/danger` (orange) | `dismiss-filled` |
| `info` (default) | `status-indicator/surface/neutral` | `info-filled` |
| `loader` | `status-indicator/surface/neutral` | A spinning ring |

Figma's `alert` is the amber step and its `warning` the orange one. Every V1.2 snack bar carries
a status tile; with no `status`, it shows `info`.

## Anatomy

- **Tile** — a 44px status tile at the start.
- **Message** — `message` in `body/md`, white. `aed` adds an amount, such as "AED 60.00", in
  SemiBold 14px right after it.
- **Action** — `action` renders a primary `sm` inverse [Button](./button) that calls `onAction`.
- **Backdrop** — `backdrop` lays a soft gradient and blur behind the pill, so it reads over busy
  content.

```tsx
<Snackbar status="success" message="Payment received" aed="AED 60.00" />
<Snackbar status="loader" message="Activating your add-on" />
<Snackbar status="alert" message="Your balance is low" action="Recharge" onAction={recharge} backdrop />
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  - `tone` is now `status`: `positive` becomes `success`, `warning` becomes `alert`, `danger`
    becomes `warning` and `loading` becomes `loader`. `tone="default"` still renders the old
    bare, centred message.
  - `subtitle` and `onDismiss` have no V1.2 counterpart; the snack bar has one line and no
    dismiss control.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [Snack-bar — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=40278-34192)

- Pill: `surface/base/midnight`, 16px radius (`border-radius/6`), padding 2/8/2/2, at least 48px
  tall, with a 10px gap that Figma leaves unbound.
- Tile: 44px at a 14px radius (`border-radius/5`), padding 6, holding a 24px filled glyph in
  `alert-message/text/info`.
- Message: `body/md` in `text/default/inverse`; the amount sits 3px after it.
- Backdrop: padding 0/16/32/16 and a 15% gradient from #3f3f3f to transparent, rising from the
  bottom. Figma's progressive blur (0 to 8) is approximated with a masked 8px blur.
