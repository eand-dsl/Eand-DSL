## When to use

- **Use** in a "New on etisalat" carousel, in a [Section](./section) of size `md`, to announce new
  services and content. Set `selected` on the last card in the carousel; Figma shows the ring
  there for three days.
- **Don't use** for offers with a price (use [DealCard](./dealcard)) or for general tiles (use
  [GeneralCard](./generalcard)).

## Anatomy

- **Frame** — `image` in a rounded frame that fills the height above the caption.
- **Badge** — the `md` "New card" badge at the top start. `badge` (on by default) is
  independent of `selected`.
- **Ring** — `selected` draws a 2px gradient ring around the frame without shrinking the image.
- **Caption** — `title` in `body/md`, centred, up to two lines.

The tile has no surface of its own; only the picture frame is rounded.

```tsx
<NewCard image={<img src={cover} alt="" />} title="etisalat money" selected />
<NewCard image={<img src={cover} alt="" />} title="Stream on etisalat tv" badge={false} />
```

## Design

Figma source: [.new-on-eand-state — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=26523-22852)

- 137 × 224 (`row-3`), with 12px (`spacing/md`) between the frame and the caption.
- Frame: a 24px radius (`border-radius/8`) and 4px padding (`spacing/xs`) around an image
  container at a 20px radius (`border-radius/7`).
- Badge at 12, 12 (`spacing/md`).
- Ring: a 37° gradient from #47cb6c through #191329 at 56% to #e00800. Its three stops are
  legacy colour styles with no V1.2 token, so they are literals.
