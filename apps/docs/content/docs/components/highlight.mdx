## When to use

- **Use** for the one or two campaigns that lead a screen, in a [Section](./section) whose
  `size` matches the banner: `xl` for `lg`, `md` for `md`, `sm` for `sm`.
- **Don't use** for a list of offers (use [DealCard](./dealcard)), for personalised suggestions
  (use [Recommendation](./recommendation)), or without artwork. With no image the banner falls
  back to a plain midnight placeholder.

## Choosing a size

| `size` | Height | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `lg` (default) | 452px | Tag, `title` and `subtitle` (two lines each), action bar |
| `md` | 224px | Tag at the top, action bar at the bottom |
| `sm` | 148px | Tag at the top, action bar at the bottom |

The `md` and `sm` sizes hide the title and subtitle, as the published sizes do.

## Anatomy

- **Image** — `image`, a URL that covers the banner, under a midnight gradient that keeps the
  text legible.
- **Tag** — `tag="logos"` (default) shows a glass row of partner `logos`; `tag="badge"` shows the
  Limited time badge, labelled by `badge`; `tag="none"` hides it.
- **Action bar** — `action={{ title, subtitle, cta, onCta }}` renders a glass
  [ActionBar](./actionbar) with an `md` [Button](./button).
- **Stacked** — `placement="stacked"` lays this banner and `items` out as 307px cards, 4px
  apart, so the next one peeks in.

```tsx
<Highlight
  image="/campaigns/summer.jpg"
  title="Summer in the city"
  subtitle="Exclusive venue deals with Smiles"
  tag="badge"
  action={{ title: 'Smiles Unlimited', subtitle: 'From AED 25', cta: 'Play now', onCta: play }}
/>
```

<Callout type="warn" title="Deprecated">
  V1.2 has one surface, image under a midnight gradient, and puts the call to action in the
  action bar. `tone`, `background` and the bare `cta` still render, but use `image` and `action`
  instead.
</Callout>

## Design

Figma source: [highlight — etisalat Consumer App DSL V1.2](https://www.figma.com/design/pzm63BTLfPfT1stcF89ILQ/e--Consumer-App-DSL-V1.2?node-id=34272-25571)

- Heights bind `row-6`, `row-3` and `row-2`; the radius is 24px (`border-radius/8`).
- Gradient: transparent at 46% of the height, `midnight/a60` at 70% and `midnight/a80` at the
  bottom.
- Padding: `lg` 16 at the top and 12 elsewhere (`spacing/lg`, `spacing/md`); `md` and `sm` 12
  all round.
- The title applies the `heading/lg` text style (24px, 110%); the subtitle is `body/md`. Both
  use `text/default/inverse`.
- Tag: each logo sits in a 48px `surface/glass/white/lg` circle. The badge is `lg` on the `lg`
  banner and steps down to `md` and `sm` with the banner.
