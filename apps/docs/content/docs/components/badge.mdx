## When to use

- **Use** `offer` for merchandising labels on cards and products: New, Discount, Mega deals,
  Limited stock, Best seller, Online exclusive, Sold out and the rest of the published set.
- **Use** `status` for a short state on a row or card: `neutral`, `positive`, `warning`,
  `danger`, `disabled`, and `neutral-inverse` on dark surfaces.
- **Don't use** a badge as a control. A selectable or removable label is a [Chip](./chip);
  a live status with a dot is a [StatusIndicator](./statusindicator).

## Choosing a size

| `size` | Height | Min width | Radius | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sm` | 16 | 40 | 6 | `badge/sm` |
| `md` | 20 | 56 | 6 | `badge/md` |
| `lg` | 24 | 64 | 8 | `badge/lg` |

`chevron` adds a trailing `chevron-right-filled`. Every published badge ships with it off.

`status="brand"` has no Figma counterpart and is kept only until design confirms its removal.
Tier badges (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze) are not built: their gradients have no tokens yet.

- Padding `spacing/2xs` × `spacing/xs` at `sm`, `spacing/xs` × `spacing/sm` at `md` and `lg`.
- Radius `border-radius/2` (6) at `sm` and `md`, `border-radius/3` (8) at `lg`; heights and
  minimum widths bind `scale/*`.
- Colours are `badge/surface/<offer|status>` behind `badge/text/<offer|status>`.
